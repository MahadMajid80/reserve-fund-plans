# Keyword research and keyword-to-page map

**Limitation:** no access to Google Search Console, Keyword Planner or a paid keyword tool. No search volumes or difficulty scores are given, because none could be measured. The evidence used is live Australian search results (October 2026) and the WA legislation's own vocabulary. Validate with Search Console data after launch.

## What the results showed

- Queries such as *10 year reserve fund plan Perth*, *strata insurance valuation Perth WA* and *strata asbestos register Perth* return mostly **informational** pages: Landgate fact sheets, LookUpStrata explainers, strata managers' help centres, industry news. Few provider service pages rank. That suggests room for a clear, specific provider page, and that searchers want explanations, so service pages keep their explanatory depth.
- WA's official term is the **"10-year plan"** for the **reserve fund** (Landgate). "Maintenance plan" and "sinking fund" are common synonyms (sinking fund is the term in other states, and the legacy FAQ URL uses it).
- Insurance searches centre on **replacement value**, **under-insurance** and **valuation frequency**.
