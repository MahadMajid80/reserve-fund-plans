16. Reserve fund wording now follows Landgate: strata companies with **10+ lots or replacement value of $5M+** must have a reserve fund and **10-year plan reviewed at least every five years**. The old references to "Designated Strata Company", s35 and s100 were removed because section numbers changed in the 2020 reforms.
17. Insurance now says strata companies must insure for **replacement value** (Strata Titles Act s97). The old "Division 4" reference was removed. Valuation frequencies are presented as **your recommendations**, not legal requirements.
18. WHS now refers to the **Work Health and Safety Act 2020 (WA)** and **WHS (General) Regulations 2022**, in force 31 March 2022, instead of "proposed Model WHS legislation" and the OSH Regulations 1996.
19. The statement that purely residential strata companies are generally not PCBUs unless they engage workers is kept from the old FAQ. Confirm.
20. Asbestos: register requirement for workplaces in buildings constructed before 31 December 2003, and management plan review at least every 5 years (WorkSafe WA). The **penalty table** now uses WA's maximum penalties as at 1 August 2026 from Safe Work Australia's comparison table (body corporate $3.5M / $1.8M / $570k; officer $680k + up to 5 years / $350k / $120k; worker $340k / $170k / $55k). These are indexed, so recheck yearly.
